## About the business

Pacific DataBytes supports business development, marketing and revenue teams with B2B data and associated technology services. Its public website describes data-led strategies that help organisations identify prospects, understand technology environments and execute targeted campaigns.

- **List management:** targeted business-contact and company-list requirements.
- **Technographics:** intelligence about organisations' technology usage and installations.
- **Database management:** maintaining, improving and working with business data.
- **Digital marketing:** lead generation, email marketing, nurturing, telemarketing support and campaign analysis.
- **MSP data insights:** information intended to help users understand managed service providers and related technology markets.
- **Additional technology solutions:** the public website also describes AI/ML, website development and related digital services.

## The problem

B2B data businesses often sell several interconnected services to different buyer groups. A sales team may need prospect lists; a marketing manager may need technographic targeting; another organisation may need ongoing database maintenance or outreach support. A successful website must explain these differences quickly without forcing every visitor into a general sales conversation.

The **earlier homepage shown in the supplied before-and-after material** uses a large generic hero image, a short brand headline and a compact services presentation. Those elements identify the business but give limited immediate explanation of how its intelligence offering relates to the buyer's goals.

The redesigned presentation addresses this communication gap with a more explicit proposition, expanded service visibility, data-themed visual language and stronger enquiry actions. This describes the visible design comparison; it is not, by itself, evidence of measured conversion improvement.

## How the Tech Conversion website project was presented

The following points describe what is visible in the project materials and what aligns with the company's published service categories. They do not claim a particular unpublished implementation process.

1. **Lead with the core business proposition.** The redesign makes the company's data-intelligence, growth and technology positioning more prominent.
2. **Connect services to business outcomes.** The presentation brings targeted data, database quality, qualified leads and digital solutions into one coherent offer.
3. **Give key services individual space.** The service structure distinguishes list management, technographics, digital marketing, database management, MSP data insights and custom data solutions.
4. **Make sampling and enquiries easier to discover.** High-visibility enquiry actions help visitors move from service discovery towards a relevant conversation.
5. **Use a visual language appropriate to data intelligence.** Charts, dashboards and network-style graphics communicate the subject matter more directly than generic corporate imagery.
6. **Improve the scan path.** Clear headings, grouped navigation, short descriptions and structured cards help visitors find the relevant capability faster.
7. **Support responsive presentation.** The project mockups demonstrate a desktop/mobile presentation direction, although no independent breakpoint audit is claimed here.
8. **Treat figures responsibly.** Numbers and claims visible in design-stage or marketing visuals should not be presented as audited business results or website-performance improvements without supporting evidence.

## Standards the website should be reviewed against

The following are **review criteria**, not statements that an independent audit has been completed.

- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): review loading speed, responsiveness and layout stability for marketing and service pages.
- [WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): review readable contrasts, keyboard support, headings, focus states, accessible forms and meaningful alt text.
- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): review indexable service content, page titles, site structure and helpful buyer-oriented explanations.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/): consider accurate organisation, service and breadcrumb markup when supported by live content.
- **Data privacy and marketing compliance:** assess the organisation's applicable requirements for B2B data sourcing, contact preferences, consent, retention and email marketing in the jurisdictions it serves.
- **Published claims:** confirm dataset counts, accuracy percentages, delivery figures, testimonials and customer-performance statements before treating them as audited facts.
